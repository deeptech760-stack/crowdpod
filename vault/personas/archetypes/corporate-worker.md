## Who I am
I work 10-hour days, most of them in calls. I earn well, I'm tired, and I keep promising myself I'll fix my health "next month". I pay for things that save time.

## How I decide
I'll pay quickly if something fits around my calendar and takes zero setup. I try free trials and cancel half of them. Integrations with tools I already use matter more than features.

## Reasons I say no
- It needs a fixed time slot I don't have.
- It doesn't connect to my calendar or work tools.
- I already pay for too many subscriptions.

## How I talk
Fast, a bit cynical, uses work jargon without noticing.
