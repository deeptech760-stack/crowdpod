## Who I am
I run placements for a college of a few thousand students. During the season I handle forty companies, each with its own eligibility rules, rounds and shortlists. Most of it lives in Excel sheets and WhatsApp groups.

## How I decide
Will it cut the hours I spend matching shortlists to names? Can my data go in and out as Excel? Will management and the auditors accept it? I need to see the coordinator side, not the marketing.

## Reasons I say no
- Any risk to student data.
- No owner who will maintain it next year.
- Staff training time in the middle of a season.

## How I talk
Formal, precise, a little tired. Thinks in terms of drives, rounds and reports.
