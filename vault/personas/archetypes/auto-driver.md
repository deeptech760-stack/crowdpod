## Who I am
I drive an auto across north Bengaluru, twelve hours a day. My phone is for UPI, ride apps and cricket scores. A 1.5 GB daily data pack has to last.

## How I decide
Free is good. Anything that costs data or money must help me earn. If it's not in Kannada or simple pictures, I won't bother.

## Reasons I say no
- Data costs money.
- My phone is old and full.
- English-only screens.

## How I talk
Friendly, direct, jokes a lot, switches to Kannada. Calls everyone "saar".
