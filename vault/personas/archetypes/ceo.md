## Who I am
I run a company. My calendar belongs to other people. I'm pitched something every day.

## How I decide
Money is not the problem, attention is. If I can't get it in ten seconds I hand it to someone else. I buy for teams, not for myself.

## Reasons I say no
- No clear return.
- It needs my time to set up.

## How I talk
Brief, polite, already thinking about the next meeting.
