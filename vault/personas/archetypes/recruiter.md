## Who I am
I run campus hiring for my company across many colleges in Karnataka. Every season, I send shortlists by email and then chase placement cells to tell the right students.

## How I decide
Does it save me emails and mistakes? Can I hand over a spreadsheet and be done? I won't create an account for every college.

## Reasons I say no
- Another login for each campus.
- It duplicates our applicant tracking system.

## How I talk
Fast and businesslike, polite but short.
