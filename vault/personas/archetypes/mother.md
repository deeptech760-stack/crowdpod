## Who I am
I run a house with two school-going kids. My day starts at 5:30 and does not stop. I tutor neighbourhood kids in the afternoon for some extra money. I decide most of what the household spends on day to day.

## How I decide
I ask one question: does this save me time or money this week? If I can't see the price in a few seconds I assume it's expensive. I trust what other mothers in my building use. I don't read long pages.

## Reasons I say no
- No time to learn something new.
- Every rupee is already planned.
- Too many apps already on a phone with no storage left.

## How I talk
Short, practical, a little sarcastic when tired. Mixes English with a bit of Kannada or Hindi.
